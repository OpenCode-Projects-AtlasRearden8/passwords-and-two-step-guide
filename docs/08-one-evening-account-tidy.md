# 8. The One-Evening Account Tidy

This is the chapter that turns a mountain into one slope you can walk in an
evening. Two hours, one focused cup of coffee or tea, everything on one map.
Do it once fully; after that it becomes a 20-minute seasonal glance.

## Before the evening (10 minutes, days earlier is fine)

- [ ] Pick both the **evening** and a **backup evening**, and put them on the
      calendar. Low energy? Do two 60-minute halves. This is an
      ADHD-compatible plan on purpose.
- [ ] Pick your password manager if you haven't: [chapter 1](01-choosing-a-password-manager.md).
- [ ] Have **paper and a pen** (the map comes from it), and that cup of tea.
- [ ] Optional but nice: print [the worksheets](09-setup-worksheets.md).

## Segment 1 — The map (20 minutes)

The single most useful object of the whole evening: a **brain dump of every
account you can remember.** Not what its password is — just what exists.

- Scan email inbox and your phone's app list for.
- Search your email for the phrases `verify your email`, `welcome`,
  `sign in`, `account created` — every hit is an account you once made.
- On paper, columns: **Account, what it's for, 2FA? (Y/N/unsure), keep/drop?**
- Sort into three piles: money-and-identity (bank, tax, email, phone,
  insurance), daily life (social, shops, subscriptions), and
  "why do I still have this?" (old forums, that one demo from 2019, a store
  that closed).

If you want a screen-side worksheet to guide it:
[Worksheets A and B](09-setup-worksheets.md).

## Segment 2 — Setup the vault (25 minutes)

- Install and open your password manager; set its **passphrase** using
  [chapter 2](02-strong-memorable-passphrases.md) (4 random words, picture
  scene, no personal anchors).
- Do the **first five accounts** — the money-and-identity pile — and paste in
  new generated passwords for each as you go.
- Turn on two-step for **email and phone number/bank first**; every other
  account follows in its own time.
- Note whichever backup codes the five accounts hand out: one copy printed,
  one copy stored (see [chapter 4](04-backup-codes.md)).

Do not migrate everything tonight. Pace beats perfection; a repeated Tuesday
rhythm is how this really gets absorbed.

## Segment 3 — Close the back doors (20 minutes)

Go page by page through the money-and-identity pile:

- [ ] **Email accounts:** recovery email and recovery phone current? Old
      roommate's email still listed? (Remove it.)
- [ ] **Turn on 2FA everywhere in the money-and-identity pile** and stash
      backup codes. Six-digit codes via authenticator app is the sweet spot;
      SMS while nothing better exists.
- [ ] **Sign out of unfamiliar sessions/devices** from the security pages
      (every big site shows a list).
- [ ] **Review app grants** ("sign in with Google/Facebook") — revoke ones
      you no longer use.
- [ ] At least one passkey if any account offers it:
      [chapter 5](05-passkeys-plain-english.md); email is a fine first.
- [ ] **Check two devices:** any computer or phone you still *use* but haven't
      signed into recently (old mac at work? register its passkeys or sessions).

## Segment 4 — The trusted-person slot (15 minutes)

- Decide who your trusted person is (or people), and how shared access
  happens *by design*: [chapter 7](07-sharing-access-with-a-trusted-person.md).
- One built-in *recovery/trusted contact* program, actually set up (Apple
  Digital Legacy, Google Inactive Account Manager, or the bank's authorized
  user).
- One conversation started ("if anything happens to me, here's where the
  folder is"). One sentence is enough for tonight.

## Segment 5 — The quiet close (15 minutes)

- [ ] **Delete** at least 3 of your "why do I still have this?" accounts.
      Their passwords shouldn't be leaking in someone's 2027 hack.
- [ ] **Export a copy of your vault** to a file, put it on the USB drive/in
      the folder with the backup codes (this is your escape-route rehearsal).
- [ ] On paper, write the **date** and the next tidy date (three months
      out). Under it, the five accounts done tonight and their status.
- [ ] Tear out this chapter's checkboxes and stick them on the fridge —
      proof to future-you that the mountain was climbed.

## The seasonal glance (every 3 months, 20 minutes)

- Any new, important account still missing 2FA? Fix one.
- Backup codes and vault export still where you remember them?
- Trusted person, passkeys, "why do I still have this?" accounts — one touch
  each is enough.

## If the evening goes sideways

Laptop dies at segment 2? Phone rings at segment 3? Guest arrives?

**Every segment stands alone.** That's not a failure — that's the plan. Do
the next segment when you next get 20 minutes; the map on your paper keeps
everything pickable up where it left off. A tidy in five small sittings is
still five sittings more than most people will ever do.
